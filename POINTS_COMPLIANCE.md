# Points System Compliance

## Closed-Loop Unidirectional Ledger
To strictly comply with Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) regulations regarding mobile money and store-of-value systems, the Kurukoo platform implements a closed-loop unidirectional points system.

### Key Principles
1. **No Cash Equivalency**: "Credits" have been renamed to "Points" in all user-facing interfaces to prevent them from being misconstrued as fiat currency.
2. **Unidirectional Flow**: Points can be earned by users through engagement and platform activity, but they **cannot** be converted back to fiat, airtime, or external services.
3. **No Direct Lead Fees from Points**: Provider lead fees are no longer deducted from Points. Instead, they are deducted directly from the provider's integrated fiat wallet (e.g., OPay, Moniepoint) via direct debit or an explicit subscription model.
4. **Subscription-Based Provider Matching**: Providers must hold an active subscription tier (paid via their fiat wallet) to receive leads. Points cannot be used to pay for subscriptions directly, though they may offer discounts (100 Points = ₦10 off).
5. **Point Usage**: Points are strictly loyalty points used to boost listings or activate Nearby Pulse ("Go Live") features.

These measures ensure that Kurukoo does not operate as an unlicensed mobile money operator or deposit-taking institution.
