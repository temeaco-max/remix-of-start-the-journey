# Kurukoo Nigeria Network Model

## Product mandate

Kurukoo is a Nigeria-first intelligent network for getting things done. The primary experience is conversation: a person states an outcome, Kurukoo understands the need, finds or coordinates the relevant network participants, and continues until the outcome is complete or a human decision is required.

The network includes users, creators, providers, businesses, transport operators, couriers, contributors, and Kurukoo agents. These participants reuse the canonical identity, skill, Economic Request, provider, agent, catalogue, notification, payment and fulfilment authorities.

## Money boundary

Kurukoo does not hold or operate a customer fiat wallet. Customer/provider money movement uses an activated regulated payment provider such as OPay, Moniepoint or another appropriately licensed rail. Kurukoo records commercial events and verified settlement evidence but does not claim payment merely because an intent or request was created.

The Kurukoo Wallet surface therefore represents provider-backed payment readiness and settlement state; it is not a new Kurukoo-issued stored-value wallet.

## Network units / Points

The existing Points engine is the implementation foundation for Kurukoo's network-unit concept. Before a public rebrand to the word `Token`, the exact Nigerian legal classification of any purchasable unit must be confirmed because the SEC's current framework expressly addresses digital/virtual assets and token issuance.

The intended product behaviour is:

- units may be purchased from Kurukoo through a verified payment flow;
- authorised Kurukoo agents may facilitate unit sales and earn a configured commission;
- limited units may be awarded for defined activities to encourage useful participation;
- units are consumed for eligible Kurukoo network access/utility such as provider lead access or selected promotional capabilities;
- units are not a fiat balance, are not redeemable for cash by ordinary users, and are not freely transferable peer-to-peer;
- units do not entitle holders to ownership, profit, interest, liquidation value or investment return;
- actual goods, services, fares and provider payouts continue to settle in fiat through the appropriate payment rail.

## Transport economics

For transport, Kurukoo coordinates passenger demand and driver availability. The passenger's fare remains a passenger/driver commercial matter and does not need to be swept through Kurukoo merely because Kurukoo created the match.

A driver can instead consume eligible network units for access to qualified ride opportunities. Kurukoo earns from network access/utility rather than automatically taking a percentage of the passenger fare. Daily, pay-as-you-go, subscription-inclusive and other access packages are commercial configuration choices using the existing commercial catalogue and ledger.

The exact fee schedule must be configured by category and validated with real driver economics before launch.

## Agent network

Kurukoo agents are a distribution and support channel, not a second financial system. An authorised agent can introduce users, support onboarding, facilitate approved unit purchases, earn a commission, and help users access Kurukoo services.

Every agent sale must correlate to a verified payment/settlement event before units are credited. Agent commission is recorded in the canonical commercial ledger and settled through the provider payout boundary.

## Catalogue model

Kurukoo's catalogue is a network opportunity catalogue, not a general-purpose eBay clone.

A catalogue source represents a person, provider, business, store, affiliate or external source that has a relationship with an offer. Product/offer records describe things Kurukoo can help a user obtain. The assistant should normally search relevant verified offers in context rather than expose an enormous marketplace inventory by default.

Examples include:

- a creator video mapped to a dress offered by a seller;
- a driver offering a ride capability;
- a restaurant item available for delivery;
- a mechanic offering a repair;
- a property agent's current listing;
- an affiliate product sourced externally.

Normal users should not automatically become unrestricted marketplace sellers. A person can contribute a relevant offer when they are an authorised seller/provider/represented party or when a controlled creator/referral flow explicitly associates them with an offer.

## Creator content commerce

Creators can connect opted-in external content, including YouTube content, to Kurukoo offers. The external platform remains the media host. Kurukoo provides context, conversation, attribution and the path from interest to a real outcome.

The preferred flow is:

`external content -> creator attribution -> Kurukoo context -> conversational request -> verified seller/provider -> payment -> fulfilment -> creator reward`

Kurukoo should not rely on scraping arbitrary content. Creator/content association is opt-in and should retain source/rights metadata.

## Nigeria-first scope

Nigeria is the launch market for this economic model. UK customer/economic behaviour is disabled for the launch product rather than maintained as a second market model. The code should preserve country fields where needed for future expansion, but default commercial product configuration, copy, catalogue, unit economics and provider assumptions should be Nigeria-first.

## Design consequence

The user-facing product should be organised around the human outcome rather than the internal machinery:

`Discover -> Ask -> Act -> Complete -> Earn`

Chat remains the primary intelligent control surface. Requests, Tasks, Topics, Catalogue, Agents, Wallet, Notifications, Points/Units and other workspaces remain supporting surfaces and should not become competing product identities.
