# Agentic fulfilment and inference policy

Kurukoo's consumer AI remains Kurukoo-controlled. Cline is an admin-only engineering/operator integration and is never a user-facing model provider.

## Inference

The canonical policy starts with cheap/local inference for simple conversation, support and presentation and escalates planning, multi-step coordination and higher-authority work when an appropriate hosted provider is configured. The model is replaceable; Kurukoo owns the agent identity, skill, instructions, capabilities, authority, evidence and commercial state.

## Skills

Skills are declarative behaviour packs. A skill specifies the mission, required context, optional context, question order, validation, matching criteria, capabilities, completion evidence and failure modes. The same chat runtime can therefore handle phone repair, rides, hotels, sellers and other outcomes without a separate chatbot for each skill.

## Compound fulfilment

A real-world outcome may contain multiple legs. A same-day phone repair may require device collection, repair and return; a hotel stay may add an airport transfer or restaurant reservation. Compound legs are durable, linked to the parent Economic Request and remain subject to canonical provider/payment/evidence state.

## Delegated representation

A user can attach a Kurukoo agent to a skill and delegate bounded authority. User instructions cannot override platform safety, payment, identity, authorization or evidence rules. Financial beneficiary remains the represented party, not the model runtime.

## Jurisdiction

Service profiles are jurisdiction-aware. They define operational assumptions and required evidence without asserting that a capability is available until provider and external-system evidence exists. Legal, tax and regulated-service rules remain jurisdictional policy boundaries.
